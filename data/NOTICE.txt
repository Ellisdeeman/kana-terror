N5 vocabulary (data/n5-vocab.json)
  Word ids are the JLPT Vocab Quest N5 indexes, 0 through 717.
  The list is the N5 deck embedded in that app (fields jp, kana, en; id = index),
  originally from https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks
  (MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 Jamie Sinclair), file src/n5.csv.
  English glosses derive from JMdict (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0):
  https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
  `rank` is an original gentle commonness order added by Kana Terror
  (0 = more common). It is not part of the study app's data.

N5 kanji (data/kanji.json)
  Characters are the N5 set used by JLPT Vocab Quest (data/kanji-n5.json).
  Readings were simplified for a typing prompt: on-readings and kun stems.
  The kanji id in an exported result is the character itself.

Audio
  Sound effects and music are synthesized in the browser for this game.
  Optional spoken readings use the device Web Speech voice (ja-JP).
  JLPT Vocab Quest audio clips are not included. That app's word audio is
  licensed only for JLPT Vocab Quest.
